# Security policy

This policy applies to every repository in the Rathkit organization that does not have its
own `SECURITY.md`.

Rathkit's projects (RTE, a rich text editor, and Pack, a bundler) are pre-release and nothing
is published yet. Reports are still welcome: early reports are the cheapest to fix.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue, pull request or discussion for a suspected vulnerability.

1. If the repository shows a **Report a vulnerability** button under its **Security** tab,
   use it. That keeps the report private on GitHub.
2. Otherwise, email [github@rathkit.com](mailto:github@rathkit.com) with the subject
   `Security: <repository name>`.

Include what you found, the steps to reproduce it, and the version or commit you tested. Please
do not include real personal data or anyone else's content.

## What to expect

This is a small project. We read every report, we will acknowledge it and tell you what we
decide, and we do not yet have a fixed response time. We will credit you in the fix if you
want that.

## Supported versions

Nothing is released yet. After a project's first release, security fixes ship in its current
release, for every user. For RTE, safety fixes are never limited to a paid plan; see
[Free and paid](https://rathkit.com/docs/free-and-paid/).
